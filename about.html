8 Consecutive World Championships

From 2014 to 2021, the Mercedes-AMG Petronas F1 Team achieved an unprecedented 8 consecutive Constructors' World Championships. Built on engineering perfection, relentless drive, and world-class teamwork, the Silver Arrows redefined performance in modern Formula One history.

Andrea Kimi Antonelli is an Italian racing driver who competes in Formula One for Mercedes. Antonelli has won eight Formula One Grands Prix since his debut in 2025.
