GEORGE RUSSELL

United Kingdom

George William Russell is a British racing driver who competes in Formula One for Mercedes. Russell has won seven Formula One Grands Prix across eight seasons. Born and raised in King's Lynn, Russell began competitive kart racing aged seven.

Kimi Antonelli

Italy

Andrea Kimi Antonelli is an Italian racing driver who competes in Formula One for Mercedes. Antonelli has won eight Formula One Grands Prix since his debut in 2025. Born and raised in Bologna, Antonelli is the son of sportscar racing driver Marco Antonelli.
